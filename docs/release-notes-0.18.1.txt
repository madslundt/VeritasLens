VeritasLens 0.18.1

Reduce uploads on slow connections with new 10- and 15-second audio windows and optional lossless FLAC compression on G2. Audio upload compression is enabled by default in Settings and preserves the captured samples. Turning it off sends WAV.

FLAC is used for Gemini inline audio and OpenAI/Groq transcription, including the transcription servers selected for chat-only providers. Custom transcription servers that explicitly reject FLAC retry with WAV and remember compatibility per server/model for the app run. Slow or unavailable encoding also falls back to WAV. OpenRouter inline audio and native Ray-Ban uploads remain WAV.

The preference also applies to live captions, transcript sidecars, shared Auto transcription, and the Settings connection test. Upload size and encoding time are available in debug events.

Permissions: no new hosts or permission categories. Requires Even App 2.2.10 and SDK 0.0.16.
